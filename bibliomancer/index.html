Frequently asked questions

How does a reading work? Type a question (optional), choose one or more books, and draw a passage. Bibliomancer picks a passage at random from the books you selected. Tap the reading to write your reflections.

Which texts are included? The King James Bible (1611), M. M. Pickthall's The Meaning of the Glorious Koran (1930), Samuel Butler's translations of Homer's Iliad (1898) and Odyssey (1900), and John Dryden's translation of Virgil's Aeneid (1697). All are in the public domain. Each passage shows its reference so you can read it in full context.

Does Bibliomancer predict the future? No. It's a tool for reading and reflection. It isn't a substitute for professional, medical, legal, financial, or spiritual advice.

Do I need an internet connection or an account? No. Everything is built in, and there's no sign-up.

Where are my questions and reflections stored? Only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy below.

How do I delete a reading? Open the reading and tap delete, then confirm. Deleting the app removes all readings.

How do I move my readings to a new phone? Readings are included in a standard iPhone backup and restore. There's no separate sync.

I found a passage with an error or a wrong reference. Thank you! Please email brian@53115163.xyz with the reference shown in the app and we'll correct it in an update.

Is it available on iPad? Bibliomancer is designed for iPhone.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

"We" and "us" mean the maker of Bibliomancer. This policy explains how the Bibliomancer app for iPhone handles your information.

The short version

Bibliomancer works entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Your questions and reflections never leave your iPhone unless you choose to share them yourself.

What the app stores on your device

To keep your reading history, Bibliomancer saves the following in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • the question you typed (if any)
  • the books you chose to draw from
  • the passage that was drawn, and the date and time
  • any reflections or notes you write

We can't access this information. It isn't sent to us or anyone else.

Permissions

Bibliomancer doesn't ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, or any other protected data, and it doesn't send notifications.

Network access

All texts are built into the app. Bibliomancer makes no network requests.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a reading, iOS opens its standard share sheet, and you choose where the reading goes (for example Messages, Mail, or Notes). What happens after that is governed by the app or service you choose. Bibliomancer doesn't send anything on its own.

Contacting us

If you tap Contact, your own email app opens a message to us. If you send it, we receive only what you choose to include, use it only to respond to you, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Third parties

Bibliomancer contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking software. Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy when you use the App Store, or if you opt in to sharing analytics with developers in iOS. Any such reports we receive are aggregated and don't identify you.

Children

Bibliomancer isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your readings stay on your iPhone until you delete them. You can delete any reading inside the app, and deleting the app removes all of its data. Your data may be included in your own iCloud or computer device backups, under Apple's terms. Because we never receive your readings, we have nothing to delete on our side.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because Bibliomancer doesn't collect personal data, you can exercise these rights directly in the app. Contact us with any questions.

Changes

If we change how Bibliomancer handles information, we'll update this policy and its date, and update the App Store privacy label before the change ships.
